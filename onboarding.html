"I built Celestique not as a tool, but as a companion for the kitchen table."

Growing up, food was never just food in my home. It was how my mother said she loved us. It was how my grandfather told stories of a land far away. Every dish carried weight — history, emotion, identity.

When I began building AI systems, I kept returning to a single question: what if AI could capture that magic? What if technology could understand not just what you want to eat, but why — and then serve it to you with the warmth of a person who truly cares?

"Celestique v2.0 is the answer. A system that streams recipes live, feels personal, and honors the ancient art of cooking in the age of intelligence."

We've built it with obsessive care — the typography, the animations, the emotional language, the nutritional depth. Every detail was deliberate. Because food deserves nothing less.

Welcome to the future of cooking. Welcome to Celestique AI v2.0.

— Sooban Talha
